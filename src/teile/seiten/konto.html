Notifications

Push on this device, reminders, board

On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work if pinn. was added via “Share → Add to Home Screen” and is opened from there (iOS 16.4 or later).

On Android phones, notifications work in Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge or Firefox – not in the built-in browser of other apps (e.g. WhatsApp, Mail). Please open the pinn. address there.

Tip for Android: add pinn. to the home screen as an app – it then opens without an address bar and notifications come with the pinn. icon.

In Chrome, tap ⋮ at the top right → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Do notifications arrive late or not at all on Android when the phone has been idle for a while? Under Settings → Apps → Chrome (or pinn.) → Battery choose “Unrestricted” and check that everything is allowed under Notifications.

Applies to all devices you are logged in on.

Completed tasks

Children are members with a kids' page or the role “Child”. You also get the notifications when someone else checks off a child's task.

Board

The message arrives about half a minute after pinning – several reactions are combined. Your own notes and replies never notify yourself.

Updates

Only for admins who may install updates: one message per new version. The bell shows the update every day until it is installed.

Pantry

One combined message in the morning when pantry items are about to expire – how many days before is set on the pantry page (slider icon).

Bin collection

Only arrives if the waste calendar (Settings → Calendar) shows a collection the next day – with the bins and whose turn it is to take them out. Children only get it when it’s their own turn.

PIN for the family dashboard

Optional – protects your profile on the iPad

On the family dashboard (e.g. the iPad in the kitchen), your profile opens with a tap on your picture. With a PIN, your code is asked for first – like on the iPhone. The PIN is optional.

Family dashboard on this device

Profile selection when opening, e.g. on the kitchen iPad

As a dashboard, this device shows the family's profiles when opened. Tapping a child opens their kids' page with tasks directly, everyone else opens their profile – with a PIN if they have set one. There are no personal notifications on a dashboard device.

Only an admin of the family can set up a dashboard.

Only an admin of the family can end this.

Manage profiles

Members and guests of the family

Each family member has their own profile. You create new people as a member – the profile is created along with it. Guest accounts have no password, only open via the family dashboard and don't see finance, documents and settings.

🙈

Menus for guests

Which areas guest accounts see

Ticked areas are visible to guest accounts. Finance, documents and settings are always locked for guests. Note: The overview shows calendar, meals and tasks together on one page – if the calendar is deselected, it is missing there too.
