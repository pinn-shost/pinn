🧩 OverviewArrange and hide tiles

Order and display of the tiles on the overview. Applies only to your profile – on all devices you are logged in on.

On phones and on the iPad in portrait mode, the tiles are stacked in this order. In landscape mode the calendar is on the left (or on the right if another large tile comes before it), all other tiles next to it, stacked.

🔭 OutlookWeekly preview and monthly finance outlook

Every Sunday from 6 pm there's the weekly preview with everything important for the family in the coming week, and on the last day of the month from 6 pm the outlook on next month's finances. Both appear once as a notice at the top and as a push notification – you can open them again here at any time.

Push notifications

Applies only to your profile. For the push notification to arrive, notifications must be activated on the device (Settings → Notifications).

👨‍👩‍👧 Family membersFamily or flatshare, name, members, child lock

Household type

In a flatshare, family members are called “flatmates” and the family becomes the “flatshare” – throughout the app and for all profiles. All data is kept. Only admins can switch.

🔒 Child lock: this member's profile cannot add, edit or delete anything (checking off works), only views everything in the settings, only sees their own tasks (incl. “All”) and has no access to finance, documents and vehicles. Admins cannot be locked.

      🛒 ShoppingMain shop, special lists and categories

      Ingredients from the meal plan that match a keyword end up on the chosen list instead of the main shop – e.g. everything from Tesco, only the mince from the butcher. They remain visible on the meal plan (with a note).

      Items are sorted into categories automatically. Put the categories in the order in which you walk through your shop – applies to all lists and devices of the family. To change a wrongly detected category, long-press the item.
