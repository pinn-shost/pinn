Kids' page

Each task only appears at its time of day – e.g. no pyjamas in the morning, no breakfast in the evening. “All day” always appears. At midnight the day starts over.

Times of day

From bedtime until morning the page only shows “Sleep well”.

Rewards

Decide what can be had for how many stars. When your child asks for something, you get a notification and confirm on the kids' page or under Finance → Pocket money. Pocket money rewards then go to the balance and are booked as an expense under “Kids”.

Rewards

How many stars a task earns (1–3) is set under “Edit tasks & times of day”.
