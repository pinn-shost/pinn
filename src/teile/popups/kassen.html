💶 Funds

Each fund has its own transactions, budget, savings, pocket money, contracts, documents and vehicles. What is in a fund is only seen by its members.

With “Split expenses”, a fund calculates who owes whom how much – e.g. for shared flats, holidays or as a couple. Shopping lists can be assigned to a fund under Lists → Edit list. “Family (all)” automatically belongs to everyone and holds contracts without their own fund.
