Best-before dates

Applies to the whole family. The push notification arrives in the morning for everyone who has turned on “Pantry expiring” under Settings → Notifications – once beforehand and once on the day itself.

Scan

Product names come from the free databases Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Pet Food Facts – queried by your own NAS, using only the barcode number. If none of them knows a product, you name it once; pinn. remembers the barcode for the whole family.

What can I cook?

pinn. assumes salt, pepper and water; ingredients “to taste” don’t count. Matching works by name – “wheat flour” in the pantry fits “flour” in the recipe. Where units fit together (g, kg, ml, l, pieces), quantities are compared too.
